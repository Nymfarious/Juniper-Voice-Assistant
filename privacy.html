Juniper Privacy Notice

Effective August 24, 2026. Juniper is a small, access-limited voice-assistance application operated by Nymfarious.

Information Juniper uses

Google sign-in provides Juniper with basic account information needed to identify an approved user. Cloud-voice requests send the text a signed-in user asks Juniper to speak to protected Firebase functions and the selected voice provider.

Information kept on your device

Profile, medical, pharmacy, insurance, scripts, customized phrases, and message history are stored in the current browser session. They clear when the session ends or when you choose “Clear private data now.” Do not use Juniper on a shared or untrusted device.

Cloud services

Juniper uses Google Firebase for authentication and protected backend functions, and may use Google Cloud Text-to-Speech or ElevenLabs to create requested speech. Provider keys remain on the backend. Juniper does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Analytics

Privacy-minimized operational events may be enabled later to report feature health. They are designed not to include spoken text, scripts, names, addresses, medical identifiers, audio, provider keys, or device fingerprints.

Your choices

You may use the built-in device voice without signing in. You may sign out, switch Google accounts, clear session data, or stop using Juniper at any time.

Contact

Questions may be sent to info.nymfarious@gmail.com.
